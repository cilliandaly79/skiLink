Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

skiLink shows you and the friends you choose on a ski map. We collect only what the app needs to do that. Your location is shared only with friends you've accepted, only while sharing is on, and you can pause it, stop it or delete everything at any time.

Who we are

skiLink is run by the skiLink team (the "data controller"). Questions or requests about your data: github.com/cilliandaly79/skiLink/issues.

What we collect, and why

DataWhyWho can see it
Email address and password (stored hashed)To sign you inOnly you
Name, username, colour, ski level, home resortSo friends recognise you and routes match your levelYou, and people you've sent or received a friend request from

The legal bases are: providing the service you asked for (account, friends, routes), and your consent (location and battery sharing, notifications). You can withdraw consent at any time in the app.

Location

  • skiLink uses your location only while the app is open.
  • Your position is sent to our server only while Share my location is on. We keep only your latest position, which replaces the previous one each time. We don't keep a history of where you've been.
  • When you pause or stop sharing, your position is deleted from our server straight away.
  • Routes are worked out on your phone.

Stored on your phone

The app keeps your sign-in session, settings, downloaded maps and recently viewed map tiles on your phone so it works with poor signal. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't use analytics.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores accounts and the data above, acting as our processor. Data may be stored outside your country under the provider's data-processing terms.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app.
  • Map and weather services: OpenTopoMap and Esri provide map images, Open-Meteo provides elevation and weather, and jsDelivr serves a code library. Your phone requests these directly, so they see your IP address and which map area you're looking at. They never receive your account details or your friends.

We never sell your data or share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

  • Live location and battery: until your next update, or until you pause or stop sharing.
  • Account, profile, friendships and daily stats: until you delete your account.
  • Deleting your account (Account › Delete my account & data) permanently removes all of the above from our servers straight away.

Your rights

You can see and change your profile in the app, pause or stop location sharing, remove friends, and delete your account. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. If you're in the UK or EU, you can complain to your data protection authority (for example the ICO in the UK, or the Data Protection Commission in Ireland).

Children

skiLink is for people aged 16 and over. We check your age at sign-up and don't knowingly hold accounts for younger children. If you think a child under 16 has an account, tell us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
